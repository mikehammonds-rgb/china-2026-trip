# Spreadsheet intake guide

You may send an existing workbook in any reasonable format. Dex will interpret and normalize it. A workbook is easiest to verify when it separates these categories into sheets or clearly labeled sections:

## Overview

Trip title, destination, start/end dates, travelers, currencies, languages, cover-image ideas, and shared-photo link.

## Daily itinerary

Date, city, start time, end time, event, address, transportation, notes, and status.

## Flights, trains, and transfers

Date, type, operator, flight/train number, origin, destination, departure, arrival, terminal/station, cabin/class, baggage, and public notes.

## Hotels

City, hotel, check-in, check-out, public address, amenities, map query, and public notes.

## Activities

City, date, activity, description, address, map query, category, booking status, and best time.

## Support and documents

Share-safe contact name, role, public phone, public URL, and document purpose.

## Private booking columns

Confirmation numbers, booking references, ticket numbers, traveler names, passport details, loyalty numbers, and payment information may remain in your source workbook for planning. They are classified as private and excluded from the public repository, archive, and dashboard.

Before deployment, Dex will present any timeline gaps or ambiguous entries for approval.
